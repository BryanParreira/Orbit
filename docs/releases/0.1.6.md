# Orbit 0.1.6

## New

- **Setup Steps for every system.** While a machine's installer is attached, a Setup Steps button (in the machine's window and on its page) shows what to choose in that system's own installer: which option to pick, which disk is the machine's, and what to do when it finishes. The disk is always the machine's own virtual disk; your Mac's drives are never visible to a guest.
